- **`SigstoreVerifier` checks the daemon release with `sigstore-verify` 0.16.0 (#749).**
  `sigstore-verify`, `sigstore-trust-root` and `sigstore-types` move from 0.15.0 to 0.16.0
  together, as the verifier takes the other two's types. The check is the same: the certificate
  chain to Fulcio, the SCT, the Rekor inclusion proof, checkpoint and SET, the release workflow's
  identity and issuer matched exactly, and the SLSA provenance predicate, offline against the
  embedded public-good trusted root, which 0.16.0 carries unchanged from 0.15.0. No source
  changes: the one breaking change in 0.16.0 is to `sigstore-trust-root`'s embedded signing
  config accessors, which this crate doesn't call.
